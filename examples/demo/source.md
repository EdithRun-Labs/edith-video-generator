# A bakery's Saturday morning

Everything here is written for this template. It is not from any book, so it can
be committed and rendered without a licensing question.

## The problem

A bakery opens at six and sells out by nine. The owner assumed the ovens were
the limit, so she bought a third one. Nothing changed. The queue still formed,
and the shelves were still empty by half past nine.

## The measurement

She counted, for one week, what each station actually did. The ovens ran
fourteen hours in total. Mixing ran six. Packaging ran three and a half. The
rest of the time the dough sat on a tray, waiting for an oven to free up.

## The fix

She did not buy anything. She changed the order of the batches so that the
slowest item, the sourdough, went in first, and the quick rolls went in last.
Oven time stayed the same. Waiting time fell from forty minutes to seven.

## The lesson

Capacity is not the same as throughput. The station that looks busy is often
not the station that is limiting you. Find the queue, not the queue's excuse.
